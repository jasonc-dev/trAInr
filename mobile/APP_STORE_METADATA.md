### Description (4000 characters max)

trAInr is your intelligent workout companion that helps you train smarter, not just harder. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced athlete, trAInr provides personalized training programs powered by AI and professional templates.

**KEY FEATURES**

**Smart Workout Logging**
Track your workouts with ease. Log sets, reps, and weights with quick-tap inputs designed for gym use. Get haptic feedback on completions and see your progress in real-time.

**AI-Powered Programs**
Generate personalized training programs tailored to your goals, experience level, and available equipment. Our AI considers your preferences to create programs that work for you.

**Offline First**
Never lose a workout. trAInr works offline, so you can log your entire session without worrying about connectivity. Your data syncs automatically when you're back online.

**Progress Tracking**
Visualize your progress with detailed analytics. Track volume, intensity, and personal records across all your exercises.

**Professional Templates**
Access professionally designed training programs for various goals including muscle building, strength, and general fitness.

**What You Can Do:**

- Log workouts offline with automatic sync
- Generate custom AI training programs
- Track sets, reps, weight, and RPE
- Create and customize workout days
- View your training history and progress
- Manage supersets and drop sets
- Set rest timers between sets

Start your training journey today with trAInr - where AI meets fitness.
